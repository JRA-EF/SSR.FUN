How to enter

  1. Create a new Reserve on SSR.FUN during the campaign, or trade Reserves created during the campaign. Only Reserves created after the start count.
  2. Sign up once at ssr.fun/#/campaign: connect your wallet, add your X handle and sign (free). That covers everything: your trades count and every new Reserve you create with that wallet is entered automatically. Only signed-up wallets are ranked.
  3. Follow @strategicsuperr on X and post your thesis with a link to your Reserve.
  4. Send feedback with the button at the bottom right of SSR.FUN.
  5. Submit on Superteam Earn with the same X profile you signed up with, and your wallet address.
