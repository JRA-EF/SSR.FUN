# Liquidity signing flow: Phantom rewrites, explicit rejections, review clock, setup auto-advance

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Author:** Joao via Claude (Opus 5.5)
- **Branch / commits:** `main` @ `afaff34` (Phantom rewrite) and the follow-up commit that adds this entry
- **Deployed to:** staging (ssr-fun-staging) and prod (ssr-fun; liquidity is still off there, DEC-0245)
- **Decision log:** none (bug fixes inside DEC-0251's flow)

## What shipped
- **Phantom rewrite (afaff34):** Phantom rewrites single-signer transactions before signing. It replaces the compute budget and adds Lighthouse guard instructions (`L2TExMFKdjpN9kozasaurPirfHy9P8sbXoAN1qA3S95`). The exact-hash check threw away the signed transaction, so liquidity setup never sent and Submit stayed greyed out. A rewrite is now accepted only when every reviewed instruction survives byte for byte, in order, with the same accounts, lookups, fee payer and blockhash. Additions are limited to Lighthouse and compute-budget instructions, and the wallet's priority fee is capped at 0.002 SOL. A rewrite of a launch SSR already co-signed is still refused, with a plain message.
- **Explicit rejections:** a JSON-RPC error on send (preflight failure, rate limit) is now recorded as `rejected`, with the RPC message and the last simulation logs shown to the user. It no longer becomes "outcome unknown". Transport failures stay `pending` exactly as before. Before the next spend, a rejected signature is checked on chain once.
- **Review clock:** setup and pool launch have a 30 s preparation deadline. The signing window (45 s launch, up to 75 s setup) starts after simulation and is capped 55 s after the blockhash fetch. The browser re-measures it on its own clock (`serverNowMs`) and shows a countdown. Locally measured, preparation takes about 1 s; clock skew and the setup window outliving the blockhash were the real risks.
- **Auto-advance:** after a submission the review follows it to confirmed, failed or expired. A confirmed setup step prepares the next one, and a completed setup re-prepares the pool launch. Every step still needs the typed word and its own wallet signature.

- `PendingLiquidity.state` gains `'rejected'`.

- **Follow-up 2026-10-20:** the first real setup transaction landed (table `Cvg5mC1bC8Rsbd88xXc22PnihUe63Xjp7CiziJHMJ4HC`, Phantom Lighthouse-guarded). The launch then failed simulation because the default 0.05 SOL cost limit capped the creator's refund to the pool payer below Raydium's 0.15 SOL creation fee. The limit now defaults to 0.30 SOL for a pool launch, a starved fee gets a plain "raise the limit" message, and every failed simulation states its reason. A read-only Mainnet preparation with that table and 9.6 USDC passes: about 0.189 SOL paid by the creator, pool payer reimbursed.
- **Follow-up 2026-10-20 (2):** the launch then hit "A fresh price is required for every backing asset". Jupiter re-stamps a price only when the asset trades, so thinly traded backing assets (LIQUIDITY's `6GmAFS...` for one) are routinely 150-250 slots old. The launch-price age limit is now 1,500 slots (about 10 min, `LAUNCH_PRICE_MAX_AGE_SLOTS`), and the error names the asset. Compound keeps 150. Six read-only Mainnet preparations passed, with oldest prices up to 242 slots.
- **Follow-up 2026-10-20 (3):** add, withdraw, lock, extend, unlock, permanent and collect still used a fixed 25 s window counted from the start of preparation, which left about 20 s to type the word and approve in Phantom. They now use the same window as the pool launch: 30 s to prepare, then 45 s to sign starting after simulation, capped by the blockhash. A read-only Mainnet add preparation on both live pools (LIQUIDITY `DadWJ...` and `C4txkP...`) takes about 0.4 s and is a single signer, 958 bytes.
- **Follow-up 2026-10-20 (4):** the first real v2 distribution landed: tx `28cgn4NY...`, 11:12 UTC, collect on LIQUIDITY. 0.609657 USDC was split 0.487726 to the creator and 0.121931 to SSR (80/20). A 10 USDC add at 13:08-13:10 was prepared five times but never sent: it failed in the browser after Phantom signed. Phantom adds Lighthouse guards to lookup-table transactions too (seen in tx `TBTNhKQy...`), and rebuilding the message can reorder or move lookup accounts, which the exact lookup comparison refused. The check now reads the real tables and compares every account by its resolved address and writable flag; without the tables it stays strict. The exact on-screen error was not captured.
- **Follow-up 2026-10-20 (5):** the first real add landed: tx `4FcwxmMH14...`, 13:46:15 UTC, on LIQUIDITY. 9.949999 USDC and 1.214935 LIQUID went in (the pool received 1.213112 after the 0.15% fee), and the treasury got 0.003449761 LP, unlocked. Phantom added 11 Lighthouse guards to a lookup-table transaction, which the resolved-account check accepted. The review UI then left a dead "Submit approved transaction" button after sending. It is now one step at a time: "Approve and send", then a waiting state with no buttons, then a confirmed notice that closes the review and refreshes the pool. A failure or expiry shows "Prepare again"; an unconfirmed send shows "Check status".
