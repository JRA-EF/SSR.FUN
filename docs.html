SSR.fun Documentation

SSR.fun is where anyone can create, launch, and trade decentralized tokenized reserves. These guides explain how Reserves and Reserve Tokens work.

  1. Getting started: what a Reserve is, what a Reserve Token is, and how minting, redeeming, launching, and managing a Reserve work.
  2. Reserve Tokens on DEXes: how a Reserve Token behaves as a standard Solana token, its on-chain properties, and how any mint resolves to its Reserve.
  3. Adding liquidity: launching a Reserve Token pool from SSR.fun, its permanent fee terms and holder share, and the risks specific to Reserve Tokens.
  4. Protocol reference: program addresses, account seeds, the Reserve account layout, fee limits and splits.
